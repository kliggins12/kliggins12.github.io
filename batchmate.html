How Batchmate works — methodology & honest math (click to expand)

What this is: Batchmate downloads official Texas Lottery draw results for every draw game and generates rolling ticket batches. The "Tightest tickets" table shows the latest picks; "Best match" is the closest that exact ticket has ever come to a real draw (e.g. 11/12 on All or Nothing is the $500 tier), "Best on" is the most recent date that match occurred, and "Data thru" is the latest draw analyzed.

How tickets are built: every game uses a sliding window of the most recent draws (the W= label, usually 4–10). Numbers or digits that appeared rarely in that window become the "rare pieces" tickets are built from, then every candidate is scored against the full draw history on wins and drought length.

  • All or Nothing adds an explicit rule engine on top — absent-number anchors when coverage gaps exist, frequency bands when they don't, odd/even signature matching, and automatic window sizing per draw session (morning/day/evening/night each get their own W=). Because a draw and its 12-number complement are equivalent, only the 1,352,078 combinations containing the number 1 are tracked.
  • Pick 3 & Daily 4 bucket digits 0–9 by frequency across the window, build candidate digit multisets from the rarest digits, and score them by box-match wins (any digit order counts) and drought across each session's full history.
  • Cash Five, Texas Two Step & Lotto Texas pick core numbers seen ≤2 times in the window plus high-frequency fillers, generate the combinations, and score on their real prize tiers (e.g. Lotto Texas match ≥4 on the post-2006 6/54 matrix).
  • Powerball & Mega Millions use the same rare-piece rolling over the current number-matrix era only (PB 5/69 + bonus 1–26 since 2015; MM 5/70 since 2020), scored on jackpot and lower-tier hits.

The honest math: Lottery draws are independent random events. Historical streaks, droughts, and frequency patterns describe the past — they do not change the probability of the next draw, and every ticket remains equally likely. Batchmate is a descriptive analysis tool for organizing number choices, not a prediction system. Please play responsibly.
